MindMigo Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026. App: MindMigo: Reel Limit Counter (Android), by Algoastics Labs.

In short: MindMigo has no account and no server. Your counts, your limit, your streak and your settings stay on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your information.

What MindMigo does

MindMigo counts the short videos (“reels”) you watch in apps you choose, shows a small counter, and shows a calm pause screen when you reach the daily limit you set.

Information stored on your phone

  • How many reels you watched each day, and in which of the apps you chose.
  • The daily limit you set, your streak, and your settings (for example theme and badge options).
  • Your answers during setup (for example your usual number of reels), used to suggest a limit.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is not sent anywhere. We cannot see it.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, account or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • No advertising ID, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled in this version.
  • No purchases or payment details: this version has no paid features.

The Accessibility permission

To count reels, MindMigo uses Android’s Accessibility service. It uses:

  • which app is in front, only for the reel apps MindMigo supports (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok);
  • when a new reel starts in those apps; and
  • where the reel area is on screen, so the pause screen covers only reels.

It does not read your messages, passwords, payments or anything you type. It does not capture or record your screen, and it does not look at apps other than the supported ones. Nothing it sees is sent off your phone.

MindMigo shows you exactly what it uses and what it never reads before you are taken to Android’s settings, and you can decline. You can turn the service off at any time in Android’s Accessibility settings; MindMigo then stops counting.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, MindMigo can show alerts on your phone, for example when you are close to your limit or if counting has stopped. These are created on your phone and are not sent through any server.

Your choices and your data

  • Export: in the app, Settings → Privacy and data → Export my data saves your data to a file in a place you choose.
  • Delete: Settings → Privacy and data → Delete my data removes everything MindMigo stores. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Permissions: you can withdraw the Accessibility and Notifications permissions at any time in Android’s settings.

Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have it. If you choose to share your weekly card from the app, the image goes only where you send it.

Children

MindMigo is meant for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how MindMigo handles information, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app when the change matters. If paid features are added later, payments will be handled by Google Play and this policy will say so before they are switched on.
