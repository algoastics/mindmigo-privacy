MindMigo Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026. Last updated 25 November 2026. App: MindMigo: Reel Limit Counter (Android), by Algoastics Labs.

In short: MindMigo has no account and no server. Your counts, your limit, your streak and your settings stay on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your information.

What MindMigo does

MindMigo counts the short videos (“reels”) you watch in Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok, and shows your count on a small counter you can turn off. When you reach the daily limit you set, it shows a Stop screen over the reels. Chats, search and posts keep working.

What MindMigo stores on your phone

All of this stays in the app’s private storage on your phone. None of it is sent anywhere. We cannot see it.

  • Your reel counts for each day and each app, and the number of ads it skipped.
  • When each counted reel started and how long you stayed on it, which gives your daily receipt (reels and time in reels) and your week.
  • A scrambled marker for each reel counted today, so the same reel is not counted twice. It cannot be turned back into the reel.
  • Which positions in the app’s reel list you have already seen today (numbers only, no text or content), so a restart does not count the same reels again. This is cleared when you delete your data.
  • Your daily limit, the limit that applied each day, your streak, and the days you paused protection.
  • Your settings, and the answers you gave when you set up (for example your usual number of reels), which are used to suggest a limit.
  • Short technical records of whether counting is working (for example when the Accessibility service was turned off).

How long it is kept

The detailed per-reel records and the technical records are deleted automatically after 30 days. Daily totals stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall MindMigo.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, account or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • No advertising ID, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled in this version.
  • No purchases or payment details: this version has no paid features.

The Accessibility permission

MindMigo needs Android’s Accessibility service to count reels and stop them at your limit. Before it sends you to Android’s settings, the app explains this and you can decline. If you decline, MindMigo still opens, but it cannot count or stop reels.

What it reads

  • Which app is open, and when a reel scrolls past, in Instagram, YouTube and TikTok only.
  • Where the reel area is on screen, so the Stop screen covers only reels.
  • A “Sponsored” label, so ads are not counted. No other text on the screen is read.

What it does at your limit

  • Shows a Stop screen over the reels.
  • Asks the reel to pause, by briefly opening a transparent screen that takes no input and exists only while the Stop screen is up. Whether the video actually pauses depends on the other app.
  • Mutes the sound while the Stop screen is up, then puts your sound back when you leave.
  • When you tap Leave reels, takes you out with a tap on the app’s Home tab, or Back or Home.

What it never reads

Messages, comments, passwords, payments or what you type. It does not capture or record your screen, and it ignores anything inside other apps. What it reads never leaves your phone.

You can turn the service off at any time in Android’s Settings, under Accessibility. MindMigo then stops counting.

Permissions MindMigo uses

  • Accessibility service: to count reels and show the Stop screen, as described above.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later asks you): optional alerts, for example when you are close to your limit or if counting has stopped. These are created on your phone and are not sent through any server.
  • Vibration: small taps when you press buttons.
  • Start after restart (and keeping the phone awake for short background work): so MindMigo can carry on counting and remind you after your phone restarts.
  • Network status: Android lets apps check whether the phone is online. MindMigo does not use it to send your data anywhere.
  • Google Play billing: present for paid features that may come later. It does nothing in this version.
  • Internet: this version does not use it to send or receive your data.

MindMigo does not use “Display over other apps”, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising ID.

Updates to how MindMigo recognises reels

Instagram, YouTube and TikTok change their screens from time to time. MindMigo can keep up by downloading a small signed settings file that tells it how to recognise reels. This version does not download it. If a later version does, it will be a plain file request: no account, no personal data, and nothing from your phone is sent with it. We will update this page before that happens.

Your choices and your data

  • Export: in the app, Settings → Privacy and data → Export my data saves your data to a file in a place you choose.
  • Delete: Settings → Privacy and data → Delete my data removes your counts, history and settings from your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes them.
  • Permissions: you can turn off the Accessibility and Notifications permissions at any time in Android’s settings.

Because there is no account and no server, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on our side.

Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have it. If you choose to share your weekly card from the app, the image goes only where you send it.

If you installed from Google Play

Google Play handles the install. Google may give us anonymous, combined statistics, such as installs and crash counts, under Google’s own policy. They are not linked to anything MindMigo stores and contain nothing from inside the app.

Children

MindMigo is meant for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top of the page. If we ever change what the Accessibility service reads, MindMigo asks for your agreement again before it counts anything. If paid features are added later, Google Play will handle the payments and we will say so on this page before they are turned on.
